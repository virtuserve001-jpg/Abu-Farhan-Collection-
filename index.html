How this page connects

  1. Peers announce themselves in the room using public Nostr relays (WebSocket, no account, free).
  2. Relays forward the WebRTC handshake (SDP) only — your data never goes through them.
  3. Every peer opens a direct, encrypted RTCDataChannel to every other peer. Edits converge with CRDTs (Yjs), so no peer needs to be the "source of truth".
